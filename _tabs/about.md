I’m a technology enthusiast with a strong passion for networking, cybersecurity, virtualization, homelab technologies, and IoT development. I hold a Bachelor of Computer Science (BCS) degree and a Master’s degree in Networking and Cybersecurity, which have helped me build a strong foundation in both the theoretical and practical aspects of modern IT infrastructure and security.

Over the years, I have gained hands-on experience working with networking technologies, virtualization platforms, Windows and Linux systems, and cybersecurity-focused environments. I enjoy building and experimenting with homelab setups using technologies such as Proxmox, virtual machines, and enterprise networking equipment to better understand real-world infrastructure, system administration, and security concepts.

Beyond networking and cybersecurity, I also enjoy working with IoT devices and embedded systems, particularly Arduino-based projects. I like exploring how hardware and software can work together to create practical automation, monitoring, and security-related solutions, while continuously learning about new technologies and innovative ideas.

This blog is where I share my learning journey, technical projects, and hands-on experiences from virtualization and server deployments to networking, cybersecurity, and IoT-related topics. My goal is to create practical, beginner-friendly content that helps others learn while documenting my own growth and experiences in the ever-evolving world of technology.
